A model you can inspect.

TVL × peer multiple × growth factor × maturity factor

First compute G, the global median of all positive live-token market-cap / TVL peer ratios. Winsorize every peer ratio into [G/4, G×4] before computing category medians. The category median is the median of its winsorized peer ratios. Blend toward G: (n × category median + 6 × G) / (n + 6), where n is the category peer count, then clamp the blended multiple into [G/3, G×3]. A category with no peers has no category median and receives the global median through the same formula. Each snapshot record is one peer; related products may share a token.

Growth factor = 1 + clamp(7d change, −60%, +120%) / 200. Maturity factor = 0.6 + 0.4 × min(age, 1095) / 1095. Missing growth uses 0%; missing listing dates use 365 days. Values round to whole dollars. Listing age measures tracking history, not launch date.

The board lists every eligible protocol the model finds. Candidates have no recorded token identifiers or market cap. The same documented category and name exclusions apply to every row: CEX, bridge, liquid staking/restaking, restaked BTC, names ending in a version marker, wrapped/staked/staking names and exchange-prefixed products are excluded. Above the floor, the watchlist is authoritative because its parent-product check used fields not carried in this snapshot. Below the floor, eligibility uses the available category and name fields; parent relationships cannot be checked independently.

Implied protocol token value is a comparative scenario, not a predicted airdrop allocation or launch probability. Plans are category-based suggestions, not verified campaigns; supported assets, fees, access and reward eligibility require checking in the official app. No reward is guaranteed.
